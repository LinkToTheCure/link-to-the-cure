A Message from Wayout Recovery Society

A note of thanks from the recovery team

We are extremely grateful for all the effort, generosity, time, and donations from everyone involved, and for being recognized within the community as a positive source of support for women struggling with addiction and their families.

It has never been about becoming rich, but about leaving the world a little better than it was.

We all deserve a chance when we are ready to change, and it doesn't have to be done alone. The world can be a cruel, dark place, and it's hard to see a way out. But sometimes we need to be broken to see the light and find its way in.

We are committed to giving every woman a chance to earn her place back and helping her along the way until she is ready to walk on her own. And if she stumbles, we will be there to help her up.

We will never give up, and we will love you until you can love yourself.

Kim Mohs
Director, Wayout Recovery Society
